## Current contract

The HathiTrust collection is a rights-aware inventory. Each volume carries source, access, rights, acquisition, and publication-eligibility metadata. Restricted or unresolved material remains metadata-only; the repository does not relicense source text. Project-created manifests and schemas are separately documented as metadata outputs.

The live Hugging Face surface and Zenodo snapshots must be generated from the same rights-filtered manifest. A metadata-only inventory DOI is distinct from any full-text or derived-text publication.

## External boundary

This document does not claim redistribution permission for individual volumes, a new DOI, or a completed public upload. Those claims require volume-level evidence, exact manifest digests, and authoritative external records.
